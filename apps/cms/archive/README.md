# Archive content boundary

This app stores structured public pages derived from reviewed source material. It does
not store or expose the original zone-office writings.

Private provenance is attached through `provenance.SourceCitation`, which links a
private `SourceRecord` to the relevant page or claim. Editors must not paste private
source-management notes into public fields.

Every public page starts with authoritative Afaan Oromoo content. English is a linked
translation and may remain pending. Canonical person names, glossary terms, dates,
geography identities, and stable routes remain unchanged across translations.

Images may only be selected through the dedicated image fields. The shared validation
service rejects images unless their provenance, permission, Afaan Oromoo caption and
alt text, and review decision are complete and approved. Public rich-text fields do
not permit image, document, or embedded-media insertion.
